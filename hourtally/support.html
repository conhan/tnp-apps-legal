Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings → Backup & restore, export a backup file and send it to yourself (email, Files or a cloud drive). On the new phone, install HourTally, open the same screen and choose Restore. Your phone's own iCloud/Android backup may also restore the app's data automatically.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, then open Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → HourTally. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → HourTally. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and never renews.

My pay looks different from my paycheck.

HourTally gives an estimate from the rates and rules you enter. Check the job's hourly rate, overtime rules and break settings. Taxes and employer-specific deductions may differ.
