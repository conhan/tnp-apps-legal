HourTally Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · Developer: tnpgroup

HourTally ("the app") is a work hours and pay tracker. It is built so that your data stays on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your jobs, shifts, pay rates and notes are stored only on your device.
  • There are no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (jobs, shifts, breaks, tips, pay rules, notes and settings) is saved in a local database on your device. We have no servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it.

Backups

  • Device backup. Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Android Backup on Android) may include the app's data, according to your device settings. That backup is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, not by us.
  • Backup files. You can export a backup file or a timesheet (PDF/CSV). The file goes only where you choose to send or save it, for example email, Files or a cloud drive.

Notifications

Clock-in and clock-out reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to us.

Purchases

HourTally Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their privacy policies; we never receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks whether you own Pro by asking the App Store or Google Play directly on your device. No purchase information is sent to us or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you turn on.

The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

HourTally is a general-audience productivity app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the jobs and shifts in the app, or by uninstalling the app. Remember to also delete any backup files you exported.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version is released.
