Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings → Backup & restore, export a backup file and send it to yourself (email, Files or a cloud drive). On the new phone, install CarTally, open the same screen and choose Restore. Your phone's own iCloud/Android backup may also restore the app's data automatically.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, then open Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CarTally. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CarTally. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and never renews.

Why is fuel economy empty or different from my dashboard?

CarTally uses the full-tank method: it needs two full-tank fill-ups to calculate economy. Partial fills are added to the next full tank. If you forgot to log a fill-up, add it with the right odometer reading (any time later), or turn on Missed a fill-up before this on the next one so the average stays correct. Car dashboards often estimate and can differ by a few percent.

How are due dates calculated?

Each reminder counts from the last time you logged that service, by months and/or distance, whichever comes first. For distance, CarTally estimates the date from how much you drove recently, so keep logging fill-ups or odometer readings for the best estimate.

I don't get reminder notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for CarTally in your phone settings and that Settings → Maintenance notifications is on in the app. Some Android phones stop notifications for apps in battery saving mode; set CarTally to "unrestricted".
