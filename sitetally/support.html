Frequently asked questions

Where are my reports and photos stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no server, and the app works without internet. See the privacy policy.

How do I send a report to my client?

Open the report and tap PDF report. Choose large or small photos, or Only items to do for a list your trades can work from, then tap Share PDF and pick email, a messaging app or a cloud drive. You can also print from the same screen.

Can I add several photos to one item and mark them up?

Yes. In an item, tap Take photo or From library as many times as you need. Tap a photo to draw arrows, circles, boxes, freehand lines or text. Your original photo is kept, so you can undo or change the marks later.

How do I do a follow-up inspection?

Open the report, tap the menu and choose Re-inspection report. Every item that is still open or not checked is copied, with its photos, into a new report dated today.

How do I put my logo on the PDF?

Open Settings → Company & logo. Company name and contact details are free; the logo (and removing the small "Created with SiteTally" footer) are part of SiteTally Pro.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings → Backup & restore and export a backup file. It contains all projects, reports and photos. Send it to yourself (email, Files or a cloud drive). On the new phone, install SiteTally, open the same screen and choose Restore.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, then open Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SiteTally. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → SiteTally. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and never renews.

The camera does not open.

Check that camera access is allowed for SiteTally in your phone's settings (iPhone: Settings → SiteTally → Camera).
