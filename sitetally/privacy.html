SiteTally Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Developer: tnpgroup

SiteTally ("the app") is a punch list, site inspection and site report app. It is built so that your projects and photos stay on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your projects, reports, items, photos, drawings, signatures and company details are stored only on your device.
  • There are no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter or capture (project names and addresses, client names, report items, photos and the marks you draw on them, signatures, your company name, contact details and logo, and settings) is saved in the app's private storage on your device. We have no servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it.

PDF reports and backups

  • PDF and CSV files are created on your device. They go only where you choose to send, save or print them, for example email, messaging apps, Files or a cloud drive.
  • Backup files. You can export a backup file that contains your data and photos. It goes only where you choose to save or send it.
  • Device backup. On iPhone, iCloud Backup may include the app's data, according to your device settings; that backup is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. On Android, the app opts out of automatic device backup because photos can be large; use the backup file instead.

Purchases

SiteTally Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their privacy policies; we never receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks whether you own Pro by asking the App Store or Google Play directly on your device. No purchase information is sent to us or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of site items when you tap "Take photo".
  • Photos: to add existing photos or your company logo when you choose them.

Photos are copied into the app's private storage on your device and are not uploaded anywhere. The app does not access your location, contacts or microphone.

Children

SiteTally is a business productivity app for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting projects, reports, items or photos in the app, or by uninstalling the app. Remember to also delete any PDF or backup files you exported.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version is released.
