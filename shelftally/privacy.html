ShelfTally Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026 · Developer: tnpgroup

ShelfTally ("the app") is an inventory count and stock take app. It is built so that your inventory stays on your phone, or in your own Google Drive if you choose. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. We have no servers that receive your data.
  • Your items, stock levels, counts, photos and settings are stored on your device.
  • Google sign-in is optional and is used only to back up your data to your own Google Drive.
  • There are no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter or capture (item names, barcodes, SKUs, costs, stock levels, locations, count sessions, item photos, your company name and logo, and settings) is saved in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose Back up to Google Drive, the app asks you to sign in with your Google account and to allow access to the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. With this permission the app can only create, read and delete its own backup files in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. It cannot see any of your other Drive files.

  • The app uses your Google account email only to show which account is connected, and the access token only to upload, list, download and delete ShelfTally backup files.
  • Backup files go directly from your device to your Google Drive. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot access them.
  • The app keeps the 5 most recent backups and deletes older ones.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Backup & restore, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete backups stored in Drive, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps → ShelfTally → Delete hidden app data.

ShelfTally's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not transfer it to others, and no human reads it.

Exports and backup files

  • CSV, Excel and PDF files are created on your device. They go only where you choose to send, save or print them.
  • Backup files. You can export a backup file that contains your data and photos. It goes only where you choose to save or send it.
  • Device backup. On iPhone, iCloud Backup may include the app's data, according to your device settings; that backup is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. On Android, the app opts out of automatic device backup; use Google Drive backup or a backup file instead.

Purchases

ShelfTally Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their privacy policies; we never receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks whether you own Pro by asking the App Store or Google Play directly on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes while you count, and to take item photos when you choose.
  • Photos: to add existing photos to items or your company logo when you choose them.

Camera images used for scanning are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded. The app does not access your location, contacts or microphone.

Children

ShelfTally is a business productivity app for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

You can delete items, locations and counts in the app, or uninstall the app to delete everything on the device. Delete Drive backups as described above, and any files you exported.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version is released.
