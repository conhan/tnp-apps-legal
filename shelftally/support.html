Frequently asked questions

Where is my inventory stored?

On your phone. There is no ShelfTally account and no ShelfTally server, and the app works without internet. If you turn on Google Drive backup, a copy is saved in your own Google Drive. See the privacy policy.

How do I count stock?

Open Count and tap Start count. Tap Scan and point the camera at barcodes: every scan adds 1. Use the + and − buttons or type a number for items you count by hand. Every scan is saved immediately, so you can leave the app and continue later.

What does "variance" mean?

Variance is what you counted minus what the app expected (the stock on hand before the count). When you tap Finish count, stock on hand is updated to your counted numbers. You choose whether items you did not count keep their stock or are set to zero.

Can I import my item list from Excel?

Yes. Save your sheet as CSV, then open Items → menu → Import CSV. ShelfTally recognises common column names (name, barcode, SKU, quantity, cost, minimum) in several languages and shows a preview before importing. Items with the same barcode or SKU are updated instead of duplicated.

How do I export my count?

Open a finished count and tap Export. CSV is free; Excel (.xlsx) and the PDF count report are part of ShelfTally Pro.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings → Backup & restore. Either back up to Google Drive (sign in with your Google account on both phones), or export a backup file and send it to yourself. On the new phone, install ShelfTally, open the same screen and choose Restore.

How do I disconnect Google Drive?

Open Settings → Backup & restore → Disconnect. You can also remove ShelfTally's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete backups already in your Drive, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps → ShelfTally → Delete hidden app data.

I bought Pro on another phone.

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, then open Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ShelfTally. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → ShelfTally. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and never renews.

The scanner does not open.

Check that camera access is allowed for ShelfTally in your phone's settings (iPhone: Settings → ShelfTally → Camera).
