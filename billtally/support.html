Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings → Backup & restore, export a backup file and send it to yourself (email, Files or a cloud drive). On the new phone, install BillTally, open the same screen and choose Restore. Your phone's own iCloud/Android backup may also restore the app's data automatically.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, then open Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BillTally. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → BillTally. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and never renews.

How do I record a partial payment?

Open the bill and tap Pay other amount. Enter what you paid and turn off Paid in full: BillTally keeps the rest due and shows "Paid X of Y". Several partial payments that add up to the full amount close the bill automatically.

Which repeat options are there?

One time, weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month (choose both days, e.g. the 1st and 15th), monthly, every 30 days, every 3 or 6 months, yearly, or custom (every N days, weeks or months). A bill due on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months. You can end a bill on a date or after a number of payments.

What does Autopay do?

Bills marked Autopay count as paid on their due date, so they never show as overdue. You still get the reminder before the due date so you can check your balance.

I don't get reminder notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for BillTally in your phone settings and that Settings → Bill reminders is on in the app. Some Android phones stop notifications for apps in battery saving mode; set BillTally to "unrestricted".
