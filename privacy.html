tnpgroup apps Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026 · Developer: tnpgroup

tnpgroup makes small productivity apps that keep your data on your device. We have no servers that receive your data, and we do not collect, store or sell personal data. Each app has its own policy, linked on the apps page.

Signing in with Google

Some apps (for example ShelfTally) let you sign in with your Google account to back up your data to your own Google Drive. They request only the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which allows the app to create, read and delete its own backup files in a hidden app folder in your Drive. The app cannot see your other files.

  • Your Google account email is used only to show which account is connected.
  • Backups go directly from your device to your Google Drive. We cannot access them.
  • You can remove an app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not transfer it to others, and no human reads it.
